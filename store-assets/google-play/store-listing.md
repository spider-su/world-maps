## Title

World Maps

## Short description

Learn countries, capitals and flags with maps, quizzes and explorer progress

## Full description

Build your geography knowledge through interactive maps and short quizzes. Explore Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania with six bundled continent maps.

Four ways to learn
• Hot & Cold: use distance feedback to work toward the target.
• Find It: locate countries from country, capital, flag or mixed clues.
• Name It: choose the country or capital that matches the highlighted place.
• Learn: explore the atlas at your own pace, select countries and read their facts.

Make practice your own
Choose a difficulty and use random or adaptive practice. Zoom and regional insets help you explore crowded areas and islands. Search the Library for countries and capitals, then open country details to learn more.

See your discoveries grow
Track local learning progress, earn XP and achievements, and personalize your explorer. Completed rounds and discoveries help you see where you have been and what to practise next.

Learn offline
Core maps, quizzes, flags and country facts work without an account or connection. Expanded country photographs are optional online content and include source credits. Pronunciation uses voices available on your device.

Choose English, Polish or Russian controls and names, and use light or dark appearance. Your core learning progress stays on your device.
